How Can We Help You?

We welcome all genuine enquiries from our users. You may contact us regarding any matter related to Muslim Bro, including but not limited to:

  • 🐞 Reporting bugs or technical issues.
  • 💡 Suggesting new features or improvements.
  • 📖 Reporting incorrect Qur'an text, translations or Islamic content.
  • 🎧 Reporting problems with Qur'an audio or downloads.
  • 🕌 Reporting Prayer Time or Qibla inaccuracies.
  • ⭐ Sharing your experience using Muslim Bro.
  • ❓ Asking questions about the application.
  • 🤝 General enquiries or constructive feedback.

Every suggestion is appreciated and carefully considered. Your feedback plays an important role in making Muslim Bro better for Muslims around the world.

Response Time

We strive to respond to all genuine enquiries as quickly as possible. While response times may vary depending on workload and availability, every email is personally reviewed.

Please be patient if a response takes longer than expected. We sincerely appreciate your understanding and support.

Helping Us Improve

If you notice any mistake in the application—whether it is related to Islamic content, Qur'an text, translations, prayer times, Qibla direction, audio, or any technical feature—please notify us immediately.

Although every effort is made to ensure accuracy, no software is completely free from errors. Your reports help us correct issues quickly and improve Muslim Bro for everyone.

Together, we can continue building an application that serves the Muslim Ummah with excellence, authenticity and sincerity.
