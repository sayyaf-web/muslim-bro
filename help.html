Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is Muslim Bro?

Muslim Bro is an Islamic application designed to help Muslims strengthen their relationship with Allah (SWT) through useful digital tools such as the Holy Qur'an, Digital Tasbih, Qibla Finder, Prayer Times, Islamic reminders and many other beneficial features.

❓ Is Muslim Bro free?

Yes. Many features are available free of charge. Additional premium features may be introduced in future updates to further improve your Islamic experience.

❓ Does Muslim Bro require an internet connection?

Some features work completely offline, while others such as Qur'an audio streaming, updates and online services require an internet connection.

❓ Can I use Muslim Bro every day?

Absolutely. Muslim Bro is designed to be your daily Islamic companion for worship, learning and remembrance of Allah (SWT).

Downloads & Offline Features

❓ How do I download Qur'an audio?

Open the Holy Qur'an section, choose your preferred reciter and download the desired Surahs. Once downloaded, they can be played offline without an internet connection.

❓ Where are downloaded files stored?

Downloaded Qur'an audio is securely stored within the application and can be accessed from the Downloads section whenever needed.

❓ Can I delete downloaded audio?

Yes. You can manage and remove downloaded audio files at any time to free up storage space on your device.

Prayer Times & Qibla

❓ Why are Prayer Times different from my local mosque?

Prayer Times may vary slightly depending on your location, calculation method and the local mosque's timetable. Minor differences of a few minutes are normal.

❓ Why isn't the Qibla pointing correctly?

For the most accurate Qibla direction, ensure that Location Services are enabled and calibrate your device's compass by moving it in a figure-eight motion. Avoid using the compass near large metal objects or electronic devices that may interfere with its accuracy.

Digital Tasbih

❓ Does the Digital Tasbih save my progress?

Depending on your selected mode and settings, Muslim Bro can save your Tasbih sessions and daily progress to help you continue your remembrance of Allah (SWT).

❓ Can I choose different Dhikr?

Yes. Muslim Bro allows you to select from several common Adhkar and may also include custom Dhikr options depending on future updates.

Troubleshooting

❓ The app isn't working properly. What should I do?

First, restart the application. If the issue continues, ensure you are using the latest version of Muslim Bro. If the problem still persists, please contact the developer with details of the issue, your device model and Android version.

❓ How do I report a bug?

Please send a detailed description of the issue to: athmanbilal16@gmail.com Include screenshots whenever possible to help us investigate the problem more quickly.

❓ Will Muslim Bro receive more updates?

Yes, In Sha Allah. Muslim Bro is actively being improved with new premium-quality features, better performance, additional Islamic resources and user-requested improvements. We encourage you to keep the app updated so you never miss the latest enhancements.
