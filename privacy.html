1. Information We Collect

Muslim Bro has been designed to respect the privacy of every Muslim who uses the application. Unlike many online services, Muslim Bro does not require you to create an account before accessing the majority of its features.

We believe that Islamic applications should collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to provide a reliable, secure and beneficial experience.

Depending on how you choose to use the application, Muslim Bro may access certain information or permissions on your device. Most of this information remains stored locally on your own device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Information You Choose to Provide

You may voluntarily provide information while using the application. Examples include:

  • Your preferred Qur'an reciter.
  • Your selected language.
  • Your application appearance preferences such as Light Mode or Dark Mode.
  • Your Digital Tasbih goals.
  • Your custom Dhikr entries.
  • Your reminder preferences.
  • Your downloaded Qur'an recitations.

This information exists only to improve your personal experience while using Muslim Bro.

Information Stored on Your Device

The application may locally store certain information including:

  • Digital Tasbih counts.
  • Completed Dhikr sessions.
  • Last selected Surah.
  • Recently played Qur'an recitations.
  • Downloaded audio files.
  • Application settings.
  • Prayer reminder preferences.
  • Favorite Qur'an reciters.

This information is generally stored only on your own device and is intended to provide convenience by remembering your preferences between sessions.

Automatically Collected Technical Information

Like most modern applications, Muslim Bro may automatically receive limited technical information from your device in order to improve application stability and compatibility.

This technical information may include:

  • Device model.
  • Android operating system version.
  • Application version.
  • Screen size and resolution.
  • Crash reports (if enabled).
  • Performance diagnostics.

This information is used solely for troubleshooting technical problems, improving compatibility with different devices and enhancing the overall user experience.

What We Do NOT Collect

Muslim Bro does not knowingly collect any of the following personal information unless you intentionally provide it to us through email communication.

  • Your passwords.
  • Your banking information.
  • Your national identification documents.
  • Your contacts.
  • Your private messages.
  • Your photographs.
  • Your videos.
  • Your personal documents.
  • Your call history.
  • Your SMS messages.
  • Your browsing history.

Respecting your privacy is part of our commitment to serving the Muslim community with honesty, transparency and integrity.

2. How We Use Your Information

Muslim Bro uses information only for the purpose of providing Islamic services that you have chosen to use. Our goal is to make the application useful, secure, reliable and beneficial while respecting your privacy.

We never use your information in ways that contradict this Privacy Policy.

Your information may be used to:

  • Display the Holy Qur'an and related Islamic content.
  • Save your Digital Tasbih progress.
  • Remember your preferred Qur'an reciter.
  • Remember your selected language.
  • Save your application settings and preferences.
  • Provide prayer reminders if enabled.
  • Display accurate Qibla direction.
  • Improve application stability and performance.
  • Fix bugs and technical issues.
  • Improve compatibility with future Android versions.
  • Respond to support requests sent by email.

Muslim Bro does not use your information for unauthorized profiling, tracking your personal activities, or selling your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Our Islamic Commitment

Islam teaches believers to fulfil trusts with honesty and sincerity. We consider every user's privacy to be an Amanah (trust). For this reason, we strive to collect only the information necessary to provide the application's features while avoiding unnecessary access to personal data.

Our intention is to build technology that benefits Muslims without compromising their dignity, privacy or security.

3. Permissions Required

Certain features within Muslim Bro require permission from your device. These permissions are requested only when they are needed and only after your approval.

Location Permission

Location permission is used exclusively for the Qibla Finder. This allows the application to determine the direction of the Kaaba from your current location.

Your location is never used for advertising purposes and is not sold to third parties.

Microphone Permission

If you choose to enable Voice Tasbih or future voice-controlled features, Muslim Bro may request access to your microphone.

The microphone is used only while the feature is active. Audio is not permanently stored or recorded by Muslim Bro.

Storage Permission

Storage access allows Muslim Bro to save downloaded Qur'an recitations, Islamic audio, application settings, Digital Tasbih history and other files necessary for offline use.

Without storage permission, downloaded content may not be available for offline listening.

Notification Permission

If enabled, notification permission allows Muslim Bro to send Islamic reminders, prayer reminders, Dhikr reminders and important application updates.

You may disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Your Choice

Granting permissions is always your decision. You may refuse or revoke any permission through your Android settings at any time. Some features may become unavailable if the required permission is denied.

4. Storage of Information

Muslim Bro is designed with a privacy-first approach. Wherever possible, your information remains stored locally on your own device instead of being uploaded to external servers.

Examples of information that may be stored locally include:

  • Your Digital Tasbih history and completed sessions.
  • Your selected Qur'an reciter.
  • Your preferred language.
  • Your downloaded Qur'an audio files.
  • Your favorite Surahs.
  • Your application appearance and settings.
  • Your reminder preferences.
  • Your custom Dhikr entries.

This information allows Muslim Bro to provide a personalized experience while minimizing the amount of information that leaves your device.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, your information remains under your control and is stored only for the proper functioning of the application.

5. Downloaded Qur'an Audio

Muslim Bro allows users to download Qur'an recitations for offline listening. These audio files are stored locally on your device so they remain available even without an internet connection.

Downloaded Qur'an files are intended solely for your personal Islamic use through the application.

The application does not monitor which Surahs you listen to, how often you listen, or the duration of your listening sessions for advertising purposes.

If you remove downloaded files or uninstall the application, the downloaded content may also be removed depending on your device's storage settings.

6. Digital Tasbih Data

The Digital Tasbih feature stores your Dhikr counts, goals and completed sessions to help you continue your remembrance of Allah (SWT) without losing your progress.

Your Tasbih information is intended only for your own personal benefit and spiritual growth.

Muslim Bro does not share your Tasbih history with other users, social networks or advertising companies.

Your Dhikr records remain private unless you intentionally choose to share them yourself.

7. Qibla Finder and Location Services

The Qibla Finder feature requires access to your device's location in order to calculate the direction of the Holy Kaaba in Makkah.

Location information is used only while providing this feature and is not intended for advertising, marketing or commercial profiling.

We do not continuously monitor your location in the background without your permission.

You remain free to disable location access at any time through your device settings. Doing so will simply prevent the Qibla Finder from determining your current direction.

8. Security of Your Information

Protecting your information is extremely important to us. Muslim Bro implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, accidental loss, misuse or disclosure.

Although no electronic system can guarantee absolute security, we continuously strive to follow industry best practices to keep your information safe.

Users are also encouraged to keep their devices protected with secure passwords, screen locks and updated operating systems to further enhance their personal security.
